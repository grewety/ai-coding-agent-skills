# Proposal

## Why

Operators still relay long prompts and repeat routine decisions between planning, approval, local delivery, and protected closeout. The integrated planning, review, and worker safeguards now provide enough evidence to automate those transitions while leaving governance decisions with the operator.

## What Changes

- Let later changes whose verified integrated parent explicitly selects `propose-to-freeze` go from a short goal or a note reference to a validated local freeze and one approval card. Preserve Goal-First and Task-Guided selection, the existing charter gate when applicable, and the uncommitted draft boundary when adoption or commit authority is absent.
- Bind short approval replies to one displayed, current, frozen identity. Keep exact approval and the bounded local execution grant separately auditable. Apply clear draft feedback directly and carry retained restrictions, evidence, qualified role bindings, budgets, and stop conditions across resumes.
- Render a compact Next-Step Footer from packaged status and preflight observations. Give the operator a short supported command or concrete choices, with a file-backed handoff when switching sessions.
- Under separate `finish` authority, watch required checks for a finite period, resume eligible ordered publication and closure chapters from exact remote readbacks, and reconcile every proven dedicated worktree from prior runs.
- Keep remote automation, paid checks, previews, and tunnels separately authorized per action. Offer post-release `sync-skills` only for a named client/profile and source after a separate approval.
- Document and test the streamlined workflow in the two canonical skills, current guides, and the repository contributor contract. Adopt it prospectively for this repository.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `openspec-integration` covers the prospective policy, short approval and resume, structured stops, protected waits, worktree retirement, and optional profile-update handoff. The existing evidence, contained review, exclusive worker, and installation capabilities provide their established interfaces.

## Impact

- Canonical `openspec-delivery` and `openspec-next`, the `openspec-development` marketplace bundle, the root contributor and owning child contracts, the workflow and operator guides, affected installation guidance, and synthetic verification. Bump both skill versions and their marketplace bundle together.
- Baseline is integrated main `eb4a23c6735e0f930a7ab5faab175f984a8d8ebf`. Items 0, 0.5, 1, 2, and 3 were established by closure PRs #42, #40, #45, #48, and #53. The OpenSpec CLI remains pinned to 2.1.1 with its supported Node floor. The proposal introduces no dependency upgrade.
- Task-Guided delivery keeps the local contract and protected-integration behavior independently reviewable in order. Its plan uses the current integrated-parent policy. The proposed adoption applies only to later changes after integration. Existing approvals and the long-form recovery route remain valid under their applicable policy.

## Authority and exclusions

- Planning can prepare a worktree, bind the one verified documentation issue, validate native artifacts and obtain a read-only approach review. A plan-only freeze requires inherited or explicit local commit authority. Plan approval is an operator decision. Native `tasks.md` is the sole mutable progress authority.
- Approval and local execution confer no publication, direct target push, force, bypass, branch deletion, release, profile write, provider approval, or next-change authority. Protected publication and terminal cleanup require `finish`. Paid/external activity, retries and temporary tunnels require their own single-run decisions. Profile sync is separate and cannot mutate a shared installed package from its running project session.
- Item 5 pre-freeze conditional approval, additional skill packages, upstream OpenSpec adapter changes, a cross-change dispatcher, new host qualification, and release/deployment of this proposal are outside scope.
