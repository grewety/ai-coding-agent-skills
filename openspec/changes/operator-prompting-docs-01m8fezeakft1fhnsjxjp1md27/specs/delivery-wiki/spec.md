# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Reviewable Change Brief template

The portable Change Brief template SHALL help adopters express a verifiable outcome and its boundaries while preserving its standard frontmatter and stable identity.

#### Scenario: Author a brief for planning review
- **WHEN** an operator starts a brief from the bundled template
- **THEN** the sample models a fail-closed alternative in the outcome, distinguishes hard invariants from adaptable legacy assumptions, and separates planning acceptance from execution acceptance
- **AND** it models outcome-oriented wording with only necessary safety or authority mechanisms and points readers to native OpenSpec for execution tasks and evidence
