# Design

## Context

Integrated main supplies charter-v1 convergence, the pinned data-only reviewer, Goal-First and Task-Guided policy, versioned P1–P8/status observations, and exclusive-worker recovery. The existing workspace helper reconciles an implementation branch, a selected closure branch and detached inventory, but cannot prove ownership of every historical registration. The evidence package already provides lossless capture, disposable gate runs, and offline closeout assertions. Item 4 connects these pieces at the operator boundary. The existing installer and reviewer transports are prerequisites with their own trust contracts.

## Goals / Non-Goals

- Carry one change's verified identity, restrictions and evidence from a short proposal to its next legal checkpoint. Keep a typical successful lifecycle to three short operator inputs with combined approval and four with plan-only approval followed by local execution.
- Preserve native planning, one writer for Git and tasks, exact-candidate review, protected integration, and independent authority for paid inference and installed-profile changes. Support a read-only inspection route and a detailed recovery route.
- Keep portable delivery policy separate from this repository's opt-in and profile test guidance. The installed upstream adapters, external dispatcher, additional skills, and new provider or pane runtime are outside scope.

## Decisions

1. **Use the integrated parent as the authority source.** Model `propose-to-freeze` as an explicit policy observation defaulting to false. Keep prior approved and legacy plans on their inherited policy. Treat `/goal draft` as an explicit request for the bounded draft review, never as a bypass of commit authority or the selected charter. For ordinary opted-in Goal-First delivery, review the independently specified outcome and constraints when parent policy requires it and avoid prescriptive method review. For Task-Guided delivery, check the ordered dependencies and authority transitions. Use native scaffold and strict/metadata validation, then read back complete raw planning hashes before and after an applicable packaged charter review. A correction reuses the fixed binding and ledger, updates newly required artifacts, and consumes the inherited correction budget. Qualify exposure and paid launch grants one run at a time under existing policy. Review readiness and a retained paid-run grant carry different authority.

2. **Use one retained decision context per displayed card.** Record the displayed outcome, gates, exclusions, full change/repository/mode/workspace, integrated policy, frozen SHA and digest, restrictions, and reply with their verified context. Recheck complete planning bytes, exact HEAD, registered ownership, card uniqueness and freshness at receipt and dispatch. Consume a reply once and invalidate superseded cards. Make interruption or partial recording recoverable: dispatch requires both verified records for a combined reply, and ambiguous or partial state grants no execution. Preserve long-form exact approval and recovery. A clear `changes:` reply authorizes the stated planning revisions within scope. Successor freeze moves approval to its own card; existing operator restrictions remain binding. Use the existing local dispatch decisions for budgets, result gates and qualified bindings. A retained role binding identifies the route and does not confer authority for a new external run.

3. **Render operator output from verified status.** Extend the existing packaged status schema compatibly, or explicitly version changed fields. Use the same observations for read-only JSON inspection, Markdown checkpoint and the compact human footer. Report state, identities, checkpoint, PR/check state, P1–P8, exact stop, invariant/restriction status, and next input with same-session or fresh-session guidance. Examples cite actual evidence paths and preserve current approval scope. For Codex document the supported `codex --approve-for-me -C "$change_worktree" --add-dir "$change_records"` launch form and dollar-prefixed skill invocations, including its workspace-write/automatic-review behavior and the exact side-effect authority of the subsequent operator input. For an existing qualified Herdr relay, document and support a separately authorized checkpoint-only rotation with file-backed handoff, predecessor timer cancellation, successor identity verification and `HANDOFF_ACK` before predecessor close. Preserve the original process/claim until settlement and stop on an unverifiable handoff.

4. **Keep closeout orchestration at the finish boundary.** Use bounded watch under the retained operator grant and reread official protection/ruleset requirements, mergeability, reviews and exact PR head at each decision. Required-check identity, observed deadlines and previous results are evidence. Optional app suites are separate observations, and an unknown automation outcome is not absence. Retain a short pause for human-required actions or terminal wait outcomes. After a check or review wait, contextual `continue` resumes only the same exact verified grant and PR. Consume existing packaged capture/seal/readback tools. Keep their offline assertions and operator-reported check fact reads independent of the orchestrator's time-bounded wait. A real applicable optional app action, deployment preview, or paid run uses a separate approval and never silently retries. Complete spec promotion before finish readiness, verify integrated gates, run full `npm test` on the actual archived closure tree before publication, and retain the separate exact closure review before merge. Exact implementation gate evidence uses the packaged `disposable-gate-run` and the `gate-run:<wrapperSha256>` acceptance binding, with no substitute for incomplete evidence.

5. **Retain per-worktree ownership observations across runs.** Extend the read-only workspace lifecycle to reconcile registered entries with canonical path, repository/change, role and ref or detached SHA evidence. Treat present ambiguous entries as preserved blockers and previously removed verified entries as already settled. Apply the existing safety predicates individually, requiring no active writer, verified exact HEAD/closure ancestry, clean tracked/untracked state and checked ignored/submodule contents before removal. Preserve branches and other checkouts. Report partial cleanup separately from archive acceptance and resume pending cleanup without retrying completed remote steps. Retain `keep the worktree` across resumes.

6. **Keep post-release sync distinct from delivery.** Present a separately authorized update choice for a selected immutable source and profile/client after integration and applicable publication. Use normal Hermes discovery and scanning for scanner-managed updates. For a reviewed native Codex copy, use the established `install:local -- --update --client codex` route from the named revision with its collision and backup rules. Match complete bytes, the current top-level and applicable upstream `metadata.version`, and load the result in a fresh session. Keep upstream backups outside discovery, never write an active shared installation from the project session, and use scratch homes/profiles for packaging, deployment and installer tests. Document repository-specific Hermes protection using write approval and disabled curator/nudges for actively edited canonical packages. Applying a curator lock or modifying live profile settings requires its own operator action.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- A short reply may look valid in the wrong context. Durable exact match, unique card, single use and replay/partial-write tests protect dispatch.
- A request for straight-through review may exceed its authority, quota or coverage. Keep the fixed packaged reviewer, paid-run boundary and typed charter limitations. In-scope High/Medium findings require resolution and re-review. Low findings remain advisory. Declared execution deferrals cite the task and gate that will prove them.
- Optional remote checks and stale observations can obscure required protection. Stop on unknown head identity, unreadable required protection, failed checks or required human decisions, and retain the latest exact readback.
- Multiple registrations or profile paths can conceal unrelated work. Positive identity and ownership, preserve-on-ambiguity and isolated tests bound both cleanup and installation risk.

## Migration Plan

Adopt in the repository's integrated contributor contract only for later changes, update the owning contracts and guides, and bump `openspec-delivery`, `openspec-next`, and `openspec-development` together. Keep the five-package inventory and separate orchestration bundle. Synchronize all affected installation, verification and rollback guidance with shipped behavior. A prospective disablement or protected revert follows the normal workflow. Retained freezes and grants keep their verified parent policy. An already running session can use the explicit exact-identity route and must read back original state before resuming.
