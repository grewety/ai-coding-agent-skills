# Pin note

## Outcome

A widget owner can keep one note at the top of the widget's note list.

## Acceptance

Pinning and unpinning affect ordering immediately. A pinned note stays pinned when the widget is reopened.

## Exclusions and dependencies

One pinned note per widget. Shared ordering and migration are outside this brief. No known prerequisite.
